Our Story

A small roadside stall that grew into one of Gujrat’s most loved spots for samosay, pakoray and morning nashta.

Humble beginnings

It Started With One Pan

In 2005, Umar Hayat set up a small roadside stall in Gujrat with one simple promise: make everything by hand, in fresh oil, every single day.

Word spread quickly. People came for the samosay — crispy outside, spiced soft potato inside — and stayed for the nashta. Today the little stall is a family shop, and the recipe has never changed. No machines. No old oil. No shortcuts.

We are still the same hands, making food the honest way, three generations of regulars later.
