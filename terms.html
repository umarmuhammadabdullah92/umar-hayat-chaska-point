Ordering With Us

Last updated: 28 August 2026

These terms cover how you place and receive orders with Umar Hayat Chaska Point, whether you send your order by WhatsApp, phone or in person at the shop in Gujrat.

Placing an Order

You can order directly at the counter, by phone (+92 346 681 6902), or by WhatsApp. When you message us your order on WhatsApp, that message is your offer to buy, and our confirmation reply is our acceptance.

Prices and Payment

  • All prices are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) and are as shown on our menu or as quoted to you.
  • We confirm the total with you before we prepare your order.
  • Payment is cash on pickup or delivery, or a method we agree with you in advance. We never take card details through this website.
  • Prices and the menu may change at any time, especially for daily specials.

Pickup and Delivery

  • We run a street-food counter and can usually prepare orders fresh while you wait, or you can tell us a pickup time and we’ll have it ready.
  • Where we agree to deliver, the delivery area and any conditions are discussed and confirmed on WhatsApp before we send the order.
  • We cook in fresh oil with halal ingredients, as we always have since 2005.

Allergies and Dietary Needs

Please tell us about any allergy or dietary need when you order. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, eggs and dairy, so while we always do our best, we cannot guarantee an allergy-free meal. Food is prepared, packed and handled as a normal open kitchen.

Quality and Feedback

If your order is not right, message us on WhatsApp and we will fix it or replace it. We want every customer to leave happy — your feedback directly shapes the way we cook.

Limitation of Liability

Our responsibility is limited to the value of the food you bought from us. To the fullest extent permitted by the laws of Pakistan, we are not liable for any indirect or consequential loss arising from your order or use of this website.

Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan and the courts of Gujrat have jurisdiction.

Contact

Questions about these terms? Message us on WhatsApp at +92 346 681 6902, or ask us at the counter — we are open every day, 6 AM to 6 PM.
