# Images

There are no photographs on the site yet. Every slot is reserved and holds
its aspect ratio whether or not a photo is in it, so dropping images in
later cannot reflow the page or move anything that is already correct.

## The slots

| File | Where it appears | Ratio | Ideal export |
|---|---|---|---|
| `img/signature-malai-boti.*` | Signature dish 1 | 4:5 | 1200x1500 |
| `img/signature-mutton-seekh.*` | Signature dish 2 | 4:5 | 1200x1500 |
| `img/signature-grilled-chicken.*` | Signature dish 3 | 4:5 | 1200x1500 |
| `img/story.*` | The story section | 3:4 | 1200x1600 |
| `img/social.*` | Link previews (`og:image`) | 1200x630 | 1200x630 |

A signature slot is keyed `signature-` plus the dish id from the `ITEMS`
list in `build.py`, so a new signature dish gets its own slot the moment
the tag is added. The three above are the dishes currently tagged
`Signature`.

## Adding a photo

Put the file in this folder using one of the names above, then:

```bash
python3 build.py
```

That is the whole procedure. `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif` and
`.svg` are all picked up, and the build takes the first extension that
exists, so you can keep a `.jpg` while you are still exporting the
`.webp`. The slot fills in on its own, with no markup to edit.

Until a file exists the slot shows a hairline and the name of what belongs
there, which reads as deliberate rather than broken.

**Photos that do not match the slot ratio are centre-cropped**, because
the slot uses `object-fit:cover`. Shoot or crop to the ratio in the table
or the edges of the dish will be cut off. A 4:5 crop of a plated dish
means shooting slightly above the plate, not straight down.

## Lighting

The site is near-black with warm light on dark surfaces. Photos shot under
cool fluorescent light will look pasted on. Match the warmth of the room:
about 2700-3200K, and let the background fall away rather than lighting
it evenly.

## The share card

`og:image` is what WhatsApp, Facebook, X and iMessage show when someone
shares a link. Until `img/social.jpg` exists it falls back to the logo,
which renders as a small, badly cropped square. This is the highest-value
image on the site and the cheapest to fix.

## Do not hand-edit the HTML

Every page is regenerated by `python3 build.py`: `index.html`,
`menu.html`, `story.html`, `private-dining.html`, `visit.html` and
`reserve.html`. Anything typed into them is overwritten on the next build.
