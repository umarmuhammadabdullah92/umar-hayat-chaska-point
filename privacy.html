Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Umar Hayat Chaska Point (“we”, “our shop”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what little information we collect when you order with us, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Our website is a simple brochure — it has no forms, no logins and no tracking, so simply visiting this site tells us nothing about you.

When you place an order on WhatsApp, you choose to share with us:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your order details and, if you ask for delivery, your address

We never ask for, or collect, payment card numbers. Payment is settled however we agree with you — typically cash or a transfer you arrange yourself.

How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm your order and take payment
  • To tell you when your order is ready, or to deliver it
  • To reply to your questions about our food and opening hours

We do not build profiles, send newsletters, or use your details for marketing. You will only ever be contacted about your own order.

Sharing Your Information

We never sell, rent or trade your information to anyone. It is only ever shared where the law of Pakistan requires us to do so.

How Long We Keep It

WhatsApp conversations remain in the app on the device you use to chat with us and on ours. You may delete the chat at any time from your side. We delete the details of an order on WhatsApp whenever you ask us to.

Your Choices

You can contact us any time on WhatsApp (+92 346 681 6902) to ask what we know about you, or to have it deleted. There is no charge, and it takes one short message.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and keep it, as always, one short link away on WhatsApp.
