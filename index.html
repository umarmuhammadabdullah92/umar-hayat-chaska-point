Our Story

Feeding Gujrat
Since 2005

What started as a small roadside stall by Umar Hayat has grown into Gujrat's most loved snack destination. Every item is still made by hand, using the same recipes passed down from the original kitchen — fresh oil, fresh batter, no shortcuts.

We believe good food should be honest. No artificial colours, no reused oil, no compromise on quality. Come as you are and leave satisfied.
