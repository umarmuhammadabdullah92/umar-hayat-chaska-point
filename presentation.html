Task 3 · Marketing Funnel

The Funnel: From Stranger to Order

Four stages. Two ads built for every stage. In total 8 ads that guide Gujrat from "never heard of us" to "ordering on WhatsApp."

Stage 1 · AwarenessCold audience in Gujrat discovers the brand
2 Ads
Stage 2 · InterestThey watch, save and engage with our content
2 Ads
Stage 3 · DesireThey want it — menu, trust and proof of quality
2 Ads
Stage 4 · ActionOne tap order on WhatsApp, ready on arrival
2 Ads

Budget rule: most money at the bottom (high intent), with retargeting moving warm viewers down one stage at a time.

Stage 1 · Awareness (Top of Funnel)

Two Ads to Get Noticed

Target: cold audience — Gujrat + 15 km, men & women 18–45, food & local interests. Goal: reach and brand recall.

Ad 1 · Video 15sReels / Feed

"Gujrat, you can almost smell it…"

Golden samosas crackling in fresh oil — shot close-up at the counter.

  • Format: Vertical video, first 2s = frying sizzle + steam
  • Message: Fresh oil, fresh batch, made to order
  • Target: Cold Gujrat audience 18–45
  • Goal / KPI: Reach 50,000 · 60%+ video viewed

CTA: Watch More → Follow Page

Ad 2 · Carousel 3 slidesFeed

"Est. 2005 — same recipe, same honesty."

Slide 1: the 2005 roadside stall. Slide 2: today's shop. Slide 3: the 4.9-star wall of reviews.

  • Format: Story carousel about the legacy
  • Message: 20+ years, no shortcuts, trusted by Gujrat
  • Target: 25–55 locals + newcomers in Gujrat
  • Goal / KPI: Brand recall · Carousel swipe-through rate 20%+

CTA: Learn More → Follow Page

Stage 2 · Interest (Mid Funnel)

Two Ads to Get Engaged

Target: people who watched our Awareness ads or follow the page. Goal: comments, saves, shares and profile visits.

Ad 1 · Short ReelRetarget video watchers

"This is what 'fresh oil every batch' actually looks like."

New golden oil poured in, empty at closing — proof, not a promise.

  • Format: Kitchen-behind-the-scenes reel, 15s
  • Message: Transparency builds trust — the #1 reason people switch
  • Target: 75% of Ad-1 video viewers (retarget)
  • Goal / KPI: Saves + shares 200+ · profile visits up

CTA: Save Post → Send Message

Ad 2 · CarouselReviews

"150+ happy customers a day. Here's what they say."

Real Google reviews + photos of the 4.9 rating and the morning queue.

  • Format: Carousel of review screenshots + counter photos
  • Message: Social proof — Gujrat already trusts us
  • Target: Engagers + lookalikes of page followers
  • Goal / KPI: Comments 100+ · link clicks to profile 300+

CTA: See Reviews → Follow Page
