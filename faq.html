Tala naan — also called tala hua naan, which means "fried naan" — is a popular South Asian street food that is especially loved in Pakistan. It is also known as besan wala naan (gram flour naan) or naan pakora: naan coated in a spiced besan (chickpea flour) batter mixed with sliced potatoes, onions, green chillies, coriander and spices, then deep-fried in hot oil until golden brown and extra crispy. It is best eaten hot as an afternoon snack with raita or tangy tamarind chutney. Ours costs just Rs. 100 and is available every day from 6 AM.

Baingan pakora — sold here as baingan ke pakore — is a classic fritter of thin eggplant (brinjal) slices coated in a spiced gram-flour batter and deep-fried until golden and crisp. Around South Asia it is also known as brinjal bajji, beguni (West Bengal & Bangladesh), baingan pakoda or simply eggplant / aubergine fritters. Ours is just Rs. 50 and goes perfectly with pudina or tamarind chutney.
